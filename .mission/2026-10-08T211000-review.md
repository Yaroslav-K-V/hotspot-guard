## Findings (what / where / why — no patches)

1. README describes the old History window. `README.md:449` still says
   History shows the 20 recent events; the branch adds an Analytics
   dashboard and export buttons to that window. A user opening History
   meets UI the manual never mentions.
2. JSON export mixes unlabeled windows. `HistoryView.swift` `exportJSON`
   packs ~30 days of samples with 7-day outages and 24-hour uptime into
   one payload with no window labels; a consumer can misread the outages
   as covering the sample range.
3. Coverage margin rests on system lines. 62.5% leans on path-monitor
   callback delivery and live `CLLocationManager` reads — green here,
   not formally guaranteed on every CI runner, with ~2.5 pts of headroom.
4. Weakest test cannot fail. `NetworkPathObserverTests.swift` asserts
   `deliveries >= 0`; lifecycle is pinned but a delivery regression is
   invisible (same class: two `expect(true)` structural asserts).
5. Semantic choice to confirm on sight. `AnalyticsStore.swift`
   `uptimeFraction` excludes portals from both sides — reasonable,
   tested, UI-labeled, but never explicitly picked by the user.
