## Assumptions log

1. Uptime = online / (online + offline); portals excluded from both
   uptime and downtime (user-confirmed scope implies "usable time").
2. Outage = maximal offline-sample run; wall-clock `end - start`.
3. Nearest-rank percentiles; per-network ties break alphabetically.
4. History persists 30 days / 5000 samples; loss of seconds-old data on
   kill is acceptable (no write batching).
5. `~/.`-scale permissions (0600/0700) suffice; no encryption (no secrets
   stored — SSIDs/timestamps only).
6. Mock Keychain via env var is dev-only (`#if DEBUG`); release unaffected.
7. Coverage margin (~2.5 pts) rests partly on system-dependent lines
   (path-monitor callback, real-manager status reads) — green here and on
   typical CI, not formally guaranteed everywhere.
